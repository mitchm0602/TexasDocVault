About This Document

When to Use
File this affidavit with the county clerk when you have not been paid for labor or materials furnished on a Texas construction project. This creates a lien against the property, which must be filed by the deadline under Texas Property Code Chapter 53 — typically the 15th day of the 4th month after the last day work was performed.
Legal Basis
This affidavit complies with Texas Property Code §53.052–§53.054. It must be sworn before a notary public and filed with the county clerk in the county where the property is located. Once filed, the lien secures your claim against the property until the debt is paid or the lien is released.
