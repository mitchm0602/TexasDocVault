About This Document

When to Use
Use this form when you are signing a lien waiver in exchange for a progress (partial) payment. Your lien rights are only released after the payment check clears. If the check bounces, your lien rights remain fully intact.
Legal Basis
This form complies with the statutory language required by Texas Property Code §53.284. Using a non-statutory form can invalidate your waiver. The notary affidavit adds an extra layer of enforceability and is accepted by all Texas county clerks.
