Lien Paperwork

Texas-specific mechanic's lien forms fully compliant with Texas Property Code Chapter 53. Protect your right to payment on every project — residential and commercial.

Texas law tip: All four statutory lien waiver forms below are required by Texas Property Code §53.281. Using non-statutory forms may invalidate your waiver. Always use the correct form for the payment type.

Lien Waivers & Affidavits

Which Lien Form Do You Need?

Conditional — Progress
Use when signing a waiver in exchange for a draw or partial payment. Your lien rights remain until payment clears.
Unconditional — Progress
Use after payment has been received and cleared. This is a full, permanent release of lien rights for the amount paid.
Conditional — Final
Signed when final payment is expected. Releases all lien rights contingent upon the final check clearing.
Unconditional — Final
Signed only after final payment clears. This permanently releases all lien rights. Do not sign until paid in full.
