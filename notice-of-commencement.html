About This Document

When to Use
File this notice with the county clerk before construction begins on a Texas project. The Notice of Commencement establishes the project timeline for lien rights and puts all subcontractors and suppliers on notice of the improvement. It is typically filed by the property owner or general contractor.
Legal Basis
This notice is filed pursuant to Chapter 53 of the Texas Property Code. While not mandatory in all cases, filing a Notice of Commencement is considered best practice as it establishes a clear record of the project start date, which affects lien priority and filing deadlines for all parties involved.
