About This Document

When to Use
Send this demand letter when you have not been paid for work performed on a Texas construction project. This letter puts the recipient on formal notice that you intend to file a mechanic’s lien if payment is not received. Sending a demand letter before filing a lien demonstrates good faith and may resolve the dispute without litigation.
Legal Basis
This demand letter references the claimant’s rights under Texas Property Code Chapter 53. While not legally required before filing a lien, a formal demand letter is considered best practice and may strengthen your position if the matter proceeds to litigation. It also provides evidence of the debtor’s knowledge of the outstanding obligation.
