Who must keep OSHA 300 logs? Most employers with 11 or more employees in construction must maintain OSHA injury and illness records. The 300A annual summary must be posted February 1–April 30 every year.

OSHA Recordkeeping Quick Guide

OSHA 300 — The Log
Record each work-related injury or illness throughout the year. Must be maintained and available for OSHA inspection within 4 hours of request.
OSHA 300A — Summary
Annual summary of all recordable incidents. Must be certified by a company executive and posted from Feb 1 to Apr 30.
OSHA 301 — Incident Report
Detailed report for each recordable injury or illness. Must be completed within 7 calendar days of learning of the incident.
Retention Requirements
Keep all OSHA records for 5 years following the end of the calendar year they cover. Our Excel workbooks make this easy.
