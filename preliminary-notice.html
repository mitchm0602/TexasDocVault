About This Document

When to Use
Send this notice to the property owner when you are a subcontractor or supplier who does not have a direct contract with the owner. Texas law requires this notice to preserve your mechanic’s lien rights. It must be sent by the 15th day of the 2nd month following each month labor was performed or materials delivered.
Legal Basis
This notice complies with Texas Property Code §53.056. Failure to send this notice can result in the complete loss of your mechanic’s lien rights. The notice must be sent via certified mail, return receipt requested, to the property owner and the original contractor.
